Privacy Policy — Gyeom Personal

Effective date: 2026-10-26

Who uses this app

Gyeom Personal is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computers, to back up and sync files in the owner's own Google account. It is not offered to anyone else.

What data it accesses

With the owner's consent, the app accesses Google Drive files in the owner's account, only when the owner runs it.

How data is handled

  • Files move directly between Google and the owner's computer. There is no separate server.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.
  • No data is collected, sold, or shared with third parties.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
